### Comprehensive Evaluation of the Internship Experience

**1. Workspace and Environment**
Throughout my participation, I have clearly felt the openness and enthusiasm of the FCJ team. The seniors are always by my side, ready to answer questions even after office hours. The workspace is organized scientifically, helping to maintain high focus. However, to enhance bonding, the organizers could consider designing more networking or periodic team-building activities so members can better understand each other.

**2. Role of Mentors and Organizing Team (Team Admin)**
The support from the mentor team is a major highlight. They do not just provide ready-made answers but always offer guidance, creating space for me to "diagnose" and fix problems myself. Additionally, the admin team provides extremely prompt support regarding resources and procedures, ensuring a seamless and favorable working experience.

**3. Practicality and Academic Alignment**
The tasks assigned are closely connected to the theoretical foundation I accumulated at university. Moreover, the real-world project opens up completely new technological aspects, offering opportunities to bridge the gap between theory and practice.

**4. Journey of Personal Capacity Building**
Beyond upgrading my professional mindset, I have been strongly forged in soft skills such as using project management tools, cross-functional teamwork, and professional communication in the office environment. The career experience shared by mentors is truly valuable preparation for my future career orientation.

**5. Organizational Culture and Collective Strength**
The environment here always promotes respect and equality. Despite facing peak project periods, team spirit is always maximized. Barriers regarding positions or intern roles seem to vanish, making me feel like an important link contributing to the team's success.

**6. Benefits and Support Policies**
The program provides reasonable stipends along with flexible working hours. Especially, the privilege of attending in-depth internal training series is the biggest plus and the most valuable benefit for me personally.

---

### Self-Reflection and Further Feedback

- **Biggest Highlight:** The most valuable experience for me is the maturity in problem-solving mindset and the feeling of belonging to an enthusiastic collective.
- **Perspective for Improvement:** The program could add short exchange sessions between different project teams to optimize cross-knowledge sharing.
- **Recommendation Level:** I would definitely recommend this program to friends, as it is an excellent "training ground" to polish both professional attitude and practical skills.

### Expanded Proposals
- I hope that in the coming time, the program will have more sample frameworks or in-depth instructional documents for the early onboarding phase so that new interns can catch up faster. 
- I am very willing and eager to continue accompanying FCJ projects in the future if a suitable opportunity arises.
